## UI and UX review (for the redesign, not changed yet)

1. **Duplicated controls.** New tab, private tab and Home are each in two or three places (toolbar, left rail, tab strip, menu). The 12-icon left rail repeats the menu.
2. **"Export Session" is the only filled button in the toolbar**, though it is a rare, expert action. It takes the eye from the address bar.
3. **Tabs sit under the toolbar**, unlike every mainstream browser, and the strip has no visible end.
4. **The menu is very long**: 30 entries, a 17-entry Page tools submenu, and duplicate routes to Tools.
5. **A fixed slogan in the status bar** ("governed • HTTPS-first • no autofill • no telemetry") is always shown. It does not change with the page, and "no autofill" now reads oddly next to SoteriaVault filling.
6. **Internal pages open with long paragraphs** (Passwords and SoteriaVault start with 60 to 90 words of grey text) before any control. Explanations belong under the controls they explain.
7. **Section headings are found by an inline style** (`h1 style='font-size:16px'`), which is fragile, and the internal-page palette is a third set of greys next to the window's and the start page's.
8. **No visible focus ring** on internal-page controls (keyboard users cannot see where they are).

Three directions for fixing these are prototyped for the owner to choose from. The chosen one is then built into `MainWindow.xaml`, `App.xaml` and the internal-page frame (`MainWindow.Shell.cs`).
